## Confirmed Results

- The Silk Road panel contains no broken `building_...` fallback names.
- The Southeast Asia subregion and Angkor market are absent as intended by the vanilla-East projection policy.
- The Lhasa market is correctly hosted by `b_potala` in province `9238`, the capital barony of `c_lhasa`. Province `9237` is `b_lhasa` and already hosts the Jokhang special building.
- The Baghdad market is intentionally hosted by `b_ukbara` in province `4795`, within `c_baghdad`, because the county-capital province `4828` hosts the House of Wisdom.
- The Chang'an market is intentionally hosted by `b_zhouzhi` in province `12218`, within `c_jingzhao`, because `b_changan` in province `10430` hosts the Giant Wild Goose Pagoda.
- Clicking a market row in the Silk Road panel does not move the camera. This also occurs for otherwise valid entries such as Lhasa and matches the vanilla GUI foldout behavior.
- `Bazaar`, `Marketplace`, and `Khrom` are intentional regional building names. They should not be normalized without a separate localization design decision.
