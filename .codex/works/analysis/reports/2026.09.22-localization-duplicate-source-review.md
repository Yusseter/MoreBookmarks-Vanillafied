  `b_dai_la` and `d_kinh_bac` identities were renamed to the projected
  `b_thang_long` and `d_thang_long` identities.

  `Thăng Long` value for each title, and added
  `d_thang_long_adj: "Thăng Long"`. The Yusseter merge retained the removed
  lines and missed the new adjective line.

## Live Correction

- Removed `d_thang_long: "Kinh Bắc"` and its old adjective.
- Added `d_thang_long_adj: "Thăng Long"` beside the surviving title value.
