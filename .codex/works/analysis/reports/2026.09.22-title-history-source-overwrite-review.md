# Title-History Source-Overwrite Review

## Scope

- Current Yusseter revision: `f0743bc736d13aa17be6e8588a372902a653db16`
- Pre-merge Yusseter snapshot: `09588cbb5212a31bc4a373b6990b2c4da1203a35`
- Shared Git ancestor: `35211ffab5d658e2b0074ef1042fe178a7a85430`
- Merged upstream source: `74a26a897e8c26e2b1a91680447a3adcffa960c6`
- Candidate input: `title_history_source_overwrite_candidates.csv` (`384` titles)

The comparison was performed at direct-entry level and kept regional and
development blocks separate. Upstream title IDs and title references were
mapped to their current Yusseter identities before semantic comparison.

## Results

- `355` titles have no unique pre-merge Yusseter delta. Their differences are
  source evolution, identical changes on both sides, or formatting-only changes.
- `48` unique local entries across `25` titles are still present in the current
  files. They were not lost during the upstream merges.
- `2` overlapping entries preserve the pre-merge Yusseter choice:
  `c_BAL_west_hyeondeok` at `860.1.1` and `c_lingzhou` at `1227.2.1`.
- `3` overlapping entries use the newer source chronology:
  `c_jianchuan` at `760.1.1`, `c_xiazhou` at `1210.1.1`, and `c_yinzhou` at
  `1210.1.1`.
- `1` overlapping entry is hybrid: `c_SUM_riak_nan_berbedur` at `750.1.1`
  retains the Yusseter liege comment but not the older source holder.

## Decision Roots

### `c_jianchuan`

The current chain has valid living holders at all `21` active bookmark dates.
Restoring the old `SEAsiaMB685` entry would replace the effective `DaliDong2`
holder between `760.1.1` and `769.11.15`. The same character is already used by
the surviving `c_xiangcheng` and `d_jianchuan` chains at `760.1.1`. This is a
history-design choice, not a safe regression repair, so the current source
chronology is retained.

### `c_xiazhou` and `c_yinzhou`

Both current chains have valid living holders at all `21` active bookmark
dates. Restoring the old `NSteppeMB388` entries would overwrite the newer
specific holder chains at `1210.1.1`; that character is already assigned to
`c_suizhou` on the same date. The source chronology is retained.

### `c_SUM_riak_nan_berbedur`

The removed `1000203954` holder line came from upstream commit
`fb3ecdb9237c51b4ddf10ebda4a3e9227ae446fc`, not a unique Yusseter history
design. The character is already assigned to `c_SUM_bengkulu` at `750.1.1`.
Restoring this one line would not repair the undefined `Indonesia*` holder chain
visible at the active bookmarks. That chain remains deferred source-native debt
under the existing upstream-error policy.

## Outcome

No live `BookmarksGit` file requires a change for this candidate set. The
`384` rows remain useful review provenance, but they must not be interpreted as
`384` lost local timelines or automatic restoration candidates.
